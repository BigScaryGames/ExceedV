**AP Cost:** 1
**Traits:** #Skill #Mind

**Roll:** Gossip or Intimidation vs the target's Resolve (or static DC 10 on mooks)

Read a fighter mid-exchange: stance, breathing, where the weight sits. On success, learn **one** of the following about the target, chosen by the GM to be the most visible: their best defense, their trained weapon category, their approximate remaining wounds, or whether they are bluffing confidence.
