**AP Cost:** 2
**Traits:** #Attack

**Roll:** Martial + Might/Dexterity vs the target's weapon roll (2d10 + Martial + their weapon's attack attribute)

Attempt to knock a weapon out of an opponent's hands.

- If the target is not [[Effect - Grabbed|Grabbed]] by you, you roll at -5, and on a failure the target may immediately make a basic attack against you as a free action.
- If you have the target Grabbed, there is no penalty and failure carries no consequence.

On success, the weapon drops in your zone. Picking up a weapon is a 1 AP interaction.
