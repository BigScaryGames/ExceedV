# Ready Reaction

**AP Cost:** 2 + Ends Turn
**Traits:** +Traits of the prepared action

Turn an action into a reaction.

1. Declare a specific trigger condition
2. Choose the attack you ready.
3. If the trigger occurs,(if you still have it) spend your Reaction and actions to execute the readied activity.  
If the trigger doesn't occur it ends at the start of your turn. 

GM may decline Readying reaction trigger or complex activations.

## Observability

Most combat readying are obvious to observers.  GMs are advised to use common sense to manage metagaming. Hidden preparations require appropriate Stealth checks or concealment.
## Examples
 (e.g., "when enemy enters reach," "when ally is attacked," "when door opens")
- **Ready a strike:** Spend 2AP, trigger "when enemy enters zone," on enemy's turn execute strike as Reaction, spend the AP for the reaction.
- **Ready a spell:** Spend 2 AP, trigger "when ally falls below half HP," cast the spell as Reaction
- **Ready movement:** Spend 2AP, trigger "when enemy charges," move as Reaction, spend AP normally.
# Notes
What is different from normal reacitons? 
Normal rections are trigger activated, but usually their triggers are more specific and don't require a preparation and extra actions spent.
