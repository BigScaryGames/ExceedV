**Roll:** Acting opposed by Observation

Present yourself as somone else. The disguise must be plausible; a roll doesn't supply with credentials and skills. E.g. attempt at impersonating a noble without any level of etiquette and smith without knowing how to hold a hammer will be a Screwed roll when in a smithy.

When Impersonating someone specific people who know the target might get a bonus depending on the level of knowledge of the other.

- **Success:** Your identity is accepted for this interaction; requests still use ordinary social rules. 
- **Critical success:** Your next failure to maintain your identity in the scene counts as a normal success.
- **Failure:** Your persona is questioned. 
- **Critical failure:** The observer recognizes the impersonation.

Routine conversation requires no reroll. Acting outside the behavioral norms for the role might lead to GM asking for a check.

When maintaining identity, a failure does not always mean an immidiate bust of identity, but people questinging if you are possessed, under some type of mundane of magical influence, or maybe you were another person deep inside all along.
