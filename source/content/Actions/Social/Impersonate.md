**Preparation:** At least 1 Breather; appearance and credentials may require additional work. **Traits:** #Social
**Roll:** Acting + Charisma opposed by Observation + Perception.

Present yourself as a particular person or role. The disguise must be plausible; a roll cannot supply missing credentials. Roll when an observer scrutinizes your identity. Someone familiar with the person gains advantage.

- **Success:** Your identity is accepted for this interaction; requests still use ordinary social rules.
- **Critical success:** Also gain +2 to your next check maintaining this identity before the same audience this scene.
- **Failure:** Your identity is questioned.
- **Critical failure:** The observer recognizes the impersonation.

Routine conversation requires no reroll. New evidence may trigger another check; repeating a failed claim requires changed circumstances.
