**Time:** 1 Shift. **Traits:** #Social #Downtime
**Roll:** Gossip + Charisma.

Name a topic and seek conversation among people who could know about it. Public information needs no roll. Otherwise, the GM sets the DC: 10 for circulating local talk, 14 for guarded information, 18 for closely held information accessible through these contacts. Inaccessible information requires different sources.

- **Success:** Learn one relevant report and an identifiable source or place to investigate.
- **Critical success:** Gain a second lead or discover where to corroborate the first.
- **Failure:** The Shift yields no useful lead.
- **Critical failure:** No useful lead; someone interested in the topic learns that you are asking.

Reports establish what people say, not whether it is true. Retrying costs another Shift and requires different contacts, new leverage, or changed circumstances.
