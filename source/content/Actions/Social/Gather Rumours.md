**Time:** 1 Shift. **Traits:** #Social #Downtime
**Roll:** Gossip

Attempt an exploratory check to learn what the people are talking about or follow the trail of a rumor to find the truth. Each attempt requies a setup and a shift of time to gather the talk.
**Finding Rumors** - rolled blindly this check determines the amount and scope of rumors you learn in your one shift. Higher result more interesting rumors.
**Following Rumors** - this check helps to find the origins of rumors and clean up the rumor of the own life it has gained. Success leads you up one chain, failure leads you horizontally, critical failure leads you astray.

>[!Note] Note for GM on setting the DC.
>The balance consists of - how willing people are to tell of of the information - *even the guarded secrets are sometimes ending up at tea-parties and get their own lives down the ladder* - and how difficult it is to get to the people who might hold said secrets. 
>
>When all people are accessible - its is DCs and numbers of checks.
>
>But there can be an issue of lack of connections and status or how many time you might need to gossip to get to the source of the information.
>Getting to the right tea parties even as a server can be a quest in itself, so use this sparingly to send your adventurers on funny goose chases. Make them do quests for information that is a third hand rumor of possible incoming war at best, and much more.
>
