**Traits:** #Skill #Social #Downtime 
Try to adjust the deal in shops and negotiations.
Roll an appropriate skill (usually Negotiations) against Negotiations, Resolve or other appropriate skill in attempt to shift the disposition for purchase.

Duration depends on subjective purchase size - smaller the purchase size shorter the negotiations - usually ranging from minutes to a shift. Rare and extreme purchases might lead to longer periods of bargaining.

Roll against seller's social skill:
- **Success:** Treat bargain as 1 step better
- **Failure:** Treat bargain as 1 step worse
Move it extra step for each 5 in roll difference.
