**AP Cost:** weapon strike +2

Attempt a [[Feint (WIP)|Feint]] or [[Shove]] check against an enemy in your skirmish. On success, you force them out: you and the target are removed from the skirmish into a duel — a new skirmish holding just the two of you — and you immediately strike, with the target [[Condition - Off Guard|Off-Guard]] against it. On failure, they resist and push you back: your momentum is broken, no strike happens, the AP is spent.
