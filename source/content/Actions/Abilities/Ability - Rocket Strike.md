WIP — the absurd apex of the shove line.

**AP Cost:** +3

As [[Ability - Home Run|Home Run]], but the launch is skyward: the target is removed from the map, flying up for a round, and falls at the end of their next turn — taking falling damage and landing Prone in the zone they left (or where the GM rules). If they cannot fly or otherwise arrest the fall, they are effectively out of the fight for a round.
