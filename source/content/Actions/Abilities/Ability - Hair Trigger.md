**AP Cost:** +0 (Ready action + Ranged Strike)

[[Ready Reaction]] a ranged strike with a trigger of "target enters my line of sight" or "target enters my zone or a zone within my weapon's range," or both. Preemptively Spend the AP of the attack before the end of your turn.

When any hostile target qualifies, make the attack. Spend the reaction only.
You don't chose the target you attack.
