**AP Cost:** -
1 Minuta at a site with passage sign, then roll Tracking. On a success you reconstruct what happened here within the last day: the GM answers three of your questions — who passed, what they did, their condition, which way they left, or similar. On a failure the site tells you nothing and the time is wasted. On a critical failure the GM may hand you one wrong conclusion you believe.
