**AP Cost:** +3

Declare when making a strike with an Impact or Unarmed weapon. On a hit, the target rolls Endure vs your attack roll, with a bonus equal to the number of their allies in their skirmish (up to +3). On a failure they are launched: moved along lines toward a zone of your choice, up to half your attack roll in distance (playtest: maybe the full roll), or into a wall or obstacle if one is closer. If the flight is not stopped one way or another, the attack deals critical damage.
