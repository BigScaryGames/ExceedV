In a stalled negotiation, propose a compromise that doesn't explicitly favor one party and pressure them to accept.

Roll Negotiating opposed by the other side's Negotiating or Resolve, whichever lower.

On success the parties take the deal, on failure the negitiations are over. 

You can attempt to manipulate targets prior to skew their perception on what is and isn't  explicitly in favor of one party. On a failure of manipulation you can't use this ability.
