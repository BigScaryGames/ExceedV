**AP Cost:** 1
**Traits:** #Move

Leave your current [[Skirmish]] safely.

You step out of melee and are no longer part of the skirmish. You stay in the same zone.

Leaving a skirmish any other way — [[Move|moving]] out of the zone, being shoved or pulled, or any effect that takes you out of melee without a Disengage — can trigger enemy reactions and abilities (e.g. [[Ability - Reactive Strike|Reactive Strike]]).
