**AP Cost:** Varies
**Traits:** #Move

Move along a Line from your zone to a connected zone.

`AP Cost = ceil((Line Distance × Multipliers from Effects) / Speed)`

You must have the full cost available — there is no partial movement along a line. Some lines also require checks or impose hazards to cross; see [[Lines and Zones Effects]].

If you are in a [[Skirmish]], leaving the zone without a [[Disengage]] first can trigger enemy reactions.
