**AP Cost:** 1
**Traits:** #Move

Join or start a [[Skirmish]] in your zone.

Pick a target in your zone and move into melee range with it. If the target is already in a skirmish, you engage everyone in that skirmish. If not, the two of you form a new one. You can engage an ally as well as an enemy — for example to shield them with your presence (see Strength in Numbers in [[Skirmish]]).

You can't be in two skirmishes at once. If you are already in one, you have to leave it first — usually with [[Disengage]].
