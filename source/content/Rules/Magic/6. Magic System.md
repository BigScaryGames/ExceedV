# 6. Magic System

> **Note:** The balance is not yet here. As per current state there will be some issues with spells having on average higher rolls vs defense rolls.  

## Core Principles

Magic can only be cast by [[Mage]]s - people with inherited or acquired magical ability.

If you want to be a [[Mage]], take the appropriate perk (requires GM permission) and select your origin spell (Basic).

Magic in Exceed can be #Attuned or not:
- **#Attuned magic:** Uses the Limit system, is persistent, requires no actions to maintain
- **#Active spells:** Require a [Casting Check]: `Magery + Wit + Bonuses` vs DC `[8 + Tier × 2]`
- A spell can be **both** #Active and #Attuned (e.g. summoning: you cast it with AP and it sustains on a Limit slot)
- A spell can be **neither** (no tag)
Magic has no fuel pool - its cost is risk: failed casts accumulate [[Strain]] until a breather is taken.

![[Strain]]

You can swap #Attuned spells during combat: 1 AP per effect swapped, or 3 AP to swap all effects.

# Spells

Spells that deal damage (unless otherwise noted) roll a number of damage dice equal to your Magery; the spell defines the die size. A spell written as `[Magery]d4` rolls 3d4 at Magery 3.

You cannot learn or cast spells of a tier above your Magery.

## Summoning & Companions

Minion summons ([[6.1 Summoning]]) are Limit-bound bodies of force that act on your turn. A loyal companion that grows with you instead eats no Limit — it eats your AP: [[Bonded Companions]].
