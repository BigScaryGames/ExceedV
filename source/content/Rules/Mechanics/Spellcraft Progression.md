# Spellcraft

Spellcraft is a Domain (skill following special progression rules). It progresses as the player learns spells and spellcraft perks.

Each level of Spellcraft unlocks the ability to learn and cast spells of the appropriate tier.

## Spell Costs by Tier

| Tier | Cost | XP to Next Tier |
|------|------|-----------------|
| 0 | 1 | - |
| 1 | 3 | 10 |
| 2 | 5 | +20 |
| 3 | 7 | +30 |
| 4 | 10 | +40 |
| 5 | 15 | +50 |

## Learning Spells

Spells contribute to appropriate attributes AND to Spellcraft at the same time. All XP spent on spells and #Spellcraft perks ([[Metamagic]] options, specializations) accumulates toward the Spellcraft tier thresholds above ("XP to Next Tier" is cumulative) — the domain is never bought directly.

Spells can require other spells as prerequisites. More powerful effects are gated behind prerequisites, not a separate cost tier.

### Requirements

Learning a spell from a library or spellbook requires:
- Magical Theory or Theology skill ≥ spell tier
- A source from which to learn

Learning from a teacher bypasses skill requirements.

### Duration

As per [[9.2 Downtime and Training||Training]], learning a spell takes 1 unmodified day per XP spent.
