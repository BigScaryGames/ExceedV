When wielding a weapon in each hand:
- You can attack with either weapon using its normal AP cost
- Off-hand attacks take -2 penalty unless the weapon is Light
- You gain access to dual-wield perks and abilities: [[Twin Parry]], [[Double Strike]], [[Ambidexterity]]

**Attacking with both weapons.** Two separate attacks (each at its normal AP cost, off-hand at its penalty), or one [[Double Strike]] — both weapons in a single attack for -2 AP total. If the enemy successfully defends against the first strike, the second misses automatically.

**[[Ambidexterity]]** removes the off-hand penalty for non-Light weapons and lets both weapons be non-Light.

**Shields:** Don't qualify for dual-wield perks/abilities unless you have **[[Multipurpose Shield]]**. You can still make normal shield boss attacks.
