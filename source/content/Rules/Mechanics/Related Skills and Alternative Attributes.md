When attempting a skill check player can attempt to do the skill in a different manner. 
"I try to intimidate him by lifting him and shouting at his face."

Such a requiest means using the Skills secondary attribute. Players are welcome to use their secondary attributes for the skill check if it fits the fantasy.

When something is dubious or not clear GM is the arbiter of such requests. No one can't jump from dexterity or do calculations from perception.

# Related skills and Getting help
When rolling a check you can attempt to amplify the result of the check using a secondary skill to get a circumstance bonus.
E.g. You are trying to open a safe that uses - some complex inner working. GM might allow you to add Physics if your Focus is Mechanical Engineering, or a different safe that uses a magical lock - here magical science would could potentially fit as an aide.


In a similar manner, for prolonged checks, if you have an allyversed in the second skill, they might help you adding their skill to the roll.

GM determines if the related skill is useful in the check, and how much one can add.
Full/Half/None of the skill.
