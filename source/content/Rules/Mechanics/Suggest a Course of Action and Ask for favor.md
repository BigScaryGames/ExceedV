TB split into 2 actions when favors will be more defined as a currency.
Players can ask for favors. 
Rolling for asking for a favor.
If favor is at or below Disposition level - roll is to not lower disposition.
If favor is above disposition level - roll is for if the other side might agree, the price for the favor, and if the asking the favor would lower the disposition. Narrative based.
# Asking for a Favor
When asking for a favor you might be asked for a return favor during the asking or after it or to provide a weakness - or debt. 

The level of disposition dictates if the asking for a favor is a transaction or not, and what will they ask in return. Higher disposition leading to a better deal.

| Disposition | Response                                |
| ----------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Negative    | Price higher than cost to them          |
| Neutral     | Win-win condition                       |
| Positive    | Less than cost, or IOU for future favor |
Negotiating a deal 
You can use social skills to improve the deal (+1 disposition for this favor). On failure with negative disposition or critical failure, the price increases.

NPCs can decline, counter offer, or accept.
