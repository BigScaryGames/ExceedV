1. **Choose a skill:** Negotiations, Manipulation, Gossip, Leadership,(first impression only), Intimidation (efficient for lowering disposition), Streetwise, or situationally appropriate skill (Smithing with a smith, Magical Theory with a mage)
2. **Choose Values Vector** or **Roleplay the interaction** -
In first case you say the value vector and surmise your point in second you roleplay and GM might decide the value vector instead.
3. **Roll:** The skill. Opposed 

The target will either change disposition or not. GM may decide your strategy is particularly effective or counterproductive.

This activity shall not be used repeatedly, as spamming it will lead to lowering the disposition. Using common sense of the game master to determine if the actions are overused.
