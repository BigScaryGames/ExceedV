Size matters when the difference is a step, not when it's flavor. Six size steps, anchored to human:

| Size | Examples | Max Wounds | Notes |
|------|----------|------------|-------|
| **Tiny** | cat, raven, sprite, homunculus | 1 | 2-3 HP per wound, armor 0 |
| **Small** | child, halfling-build, warhound | 1-2 | |
| **Human** | humans and humanoids | 2 | the baseline all math assumes |
| **Large** | horse, ogre, bull | 3-4 | |
| **Huge** | giant, wyvern, troll elder | 5-6 | |
| **Colossal** | dragon, kraken, siege golem | 8+ | GM-scale boss territory |

Health math stays the same ([[HP And Wounds]]): Stamina = (Armor + Endurance) × Max Wounds, Health = 5 HP per wound. Big monsters simply carry more wounds — that is the whole trick.

## Hitting Bigger Things
- **Large and bigger** creatures are big targets: attacks against them gain **+1 #Size** per step above Human (cap +2). Their Deflect suffers the same −1 per step — bulk is easy to clip but hard to stop.
- **Small and Tiny** defenders are hard to pin: attacks against them take **−1** (Small) or **−2** (Tiny) unless the attack is #Burst, #AoE, or the attacker is also Small or smaller.
- Reach: a Large creature typically strikes without Engaging, per the GM's read of its build — it counts for [[Skirmish|numeric advantage]] from outside the skirmish.

## Weight of Numbers
- **Large** counts as **2 combatants** for numeric advantage and back-to-back. **Huge** counts as **3**. A Huge thing standing its ground *is* a shield wall.
- A creature 2+ size steps above you cannot be Tripped or Shoved by you alone (multiple attackers or grappling perks may change the GM's mind). Conversely, you gain advantage on Trip/Shove against creatures 2+ steps below you.
- Grappling a creature 2+ steps larger is impossible; grappling one step larger is at a disadvantage (the Perks/BattlePerks/Wrestling/ tree applies).

## Small Nasties
Small and Tiny creatures with 1 wound die like they lived: fast. To keep swarms usable, run them with the [[NPC and Encounter Builder|simplified enemy sheet]] (static DCs) and let #Burst and #AoE attacks hit every member of a swarm in the zone for full dice.

## Design Notes
- #Size is a listed bonus type ([[Bonus Types]]): a creature's size modifiers never stack with other #Size effects — take the largest.
- Bounded accuracy holds: size never exceeds ±2 on a roll; it buys wounds, numeric weight, and reach instead.
- Playtest knob: if Huge+ bosses feel like damage sponges, cut Max Wounds and add armor (ablation) instead — armor makes the fight interactive, wounds just make it long.
