During the first impression, GM takes into account who players are - looks, reputation the NPC might know, and their traits (like Suspicious or Forgiving) This sets the initial disposition.

Some circumstances - e.g. being famous, involved in rumours, and alike - might lead to them having initial disposition before the first encounter.

Players may attempt to use their skills like Etiquette or any other appropriate one to affect the first impression.

This is purposefully left vague and up to GM interpretation.
