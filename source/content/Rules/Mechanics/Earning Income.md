# Earning Income to be done in Milestone 9.
Now the placeholder is connected with #Livelihood Skills
Depending on their complexity and level you can earn a salary, as well as region's stats - i.e. GM modifies based on situational things like market or regional wealth.
Daily salary in silver.
**Normal earning distribution chart for job market**

| Complexity | 1   | 2   | 3   | 4   | 5   |
| ---------- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Easy       | 1/5 | 1   | 2   | 5   | 10  |
| Average    | 2   | 5   | 10  | 25  | 50  |
| Hard       | 10  | 25  | 50  | 125 | 250 |
Usual modifiers are 2x for emergency or unplanned services.
5x or more for hazardous occupation. 

Bas trained person (easy 2-3) usually earns 1-2 silver a day. 

Average and Hard skills require more expensive training or contain guarded secrets behind guild memberships and **apprentice** systems, that allow for a better living, thus are coveted. 
Easy-1 is a job that can be performed by almost anyone with functioning body and a day or less of training.

Two Silver is per [[Earning Income]] an Average apprentice daily added value production on market assuming efficient productivity.

Labor usally, as a rule of thumb consists of half of the cost to make tier 1+ items.
Weaponsmith 3 (Average) is creating 10 silver worth of production a day, so according to the prices it will take him 10 days of work to make that weapon. 
out of 200 cost of the sword 100 is the labor cost, other is production and material cost that keeps the place afloat. 

Magical item creation is Hard. so to create a 0 scroll a tier 2 enchater would require 5 production out of his 25 daily, meaning he could make 5 scrolls per day.
A tier 1 could only make 2. (spending other half on production cost)

While a wand that holds a spell would require him to spend 2500 production, i.e. 100 days or a season.

Other skills more often require setting up operations, but availability of jobs for a skill depends on the location and circumstances, i.e. #GM.
