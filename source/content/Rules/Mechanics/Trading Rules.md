# Trading Rules

Most items have a listed price in the rulebook, which is the market selling price. 
## Base Prices

| Transaction   | Price Range          |
| ------------- | -------------------- |
| Shops sell at | 100% of listed price |
| Shops buy at  | 50% of listed price  |
## Bargaining
By default it is assumed that you did your initial round of back and forth, and are not offered an unreasonable deal, unless GM specifically mentions that the deal is skewed or predatory. 
If players want to roleplay bargaining take their offer suggestions and roll use the base [[Social Mechanics]] rules to determine the disposition, but instead of Suggesting use [[Bargain]] action in attempt to improve the deal. GM can give players advantage/ disadvantage or screw them as the result of their roleplay actions. 
![[Bargain]]

Bargaining and disposition shift the ranges:
- **Buying:** 75-125% of listed price 
- **Selling:** 25-75% of listed price 
- **Modifier:** ±5% per disposition level
**Tags:** #Downtime 

Note to GMs:
GMs are heavily discouraged to try to give bad prices to the players or otherwise conning them during equipment purchases, that leads to slowing down of the pace of the game. Keep it reserved for special events.
