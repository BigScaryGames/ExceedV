## Critical Results
Doubles on the kept dice modify outcomes. With advantage you choose which 2 of the 3 dice to keep — so you may keep a matching pair on purpose. With disadvantage you must keep the 2 lowest dice. On a normal roll, both dice count.
- **Success + Doubles:** Critical Success
- **Failure + Doubles:** Critical Failure (if applicable)

## Two Paths to a Critical
A critical is achieved by **doubles on the kept dice** (above) or by an **extended crit range** — perks that let you crit by beating the opposing roll or DC by 10+. Both paths produce the same critical result; they never stack into anything bigger. Doubles work for everyone; extended crit ranges must be earned (see below).

## Critical Hits — Weapons
Critically succeeding on an attack is a critical hit: **double the damage dice** (not the flat Strength or bonuses; critical doubles on the damage roll still apply).

**Extended crit range (unified logic):** if your attack roll beats the defender's roll by **10 or more**, the hit is a critical hit even without doubles. Blades training grants this by default (beat a defense by 10+ = crit). Other weapon families can reach it through their tier-3 perks; each weapon tree defines which technique carries it.

## Critical Casts — Spells
Critically succeeding on a [[Strain|Casting Check]] is a critical cast:
- **Damage spells:** double the `[Magery]` damage dice.
- **Healing spells:** double the healing dice.
- **Any other spell:** the caster picks one refinement — the duration moves one step up the [[9.1 Time and Travel|time ladder]], or the spell affects one additional willing target in your zone.

Beating the casting DC by **10 or more** is also a critical cast (extended crit range applies to casting checks the same way).

## Critical Defenses
Critically succeeding on a defense is a critical defense. By default it has no built-in effect beyond succeeding beautifully — perks and spells may add effects on own critical defenses or on an enemy's critical failures (the Predictive line does exactly this).

## Design Notes
- Criticals are a spice, not a meal: the default effect (double damage dice) is the only universal one; everything else lives on perk cards.
- Future hook (MS8 balancing): per-weapon **CritX** — X extra damage per damage die on a crit — may replace the flat double-dice default for exotic weapons. Not in use.
- Playtest knob: if crits feel too frequent at high Advantage usage, gate extended crit ranges behind doubles too (both required).
