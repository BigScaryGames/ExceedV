Disposition has raw value and level following the math logic of attributes but simplified.

| Points | Level | Name        | Negative    | Naming     |
| ------ | ----- | ----------- | ----------- | ---------- |
| -1     | 0     | Indifferent | -2          | Wary       |
| 1      | 1     | Civil       | -2          | Disliked   |
| 3      | 2     | Friendly    | -3          | Unfriendly |
| 6      | 3     | Helpful     | -4          | Hated      |
| 10     | 4     | Confidant   | -5 and less | Hostile    |
| 15     | 5     | Devoted     |             |            |

Each level makes next harder to attain, not only with points count, but quality of acts required to get to the next level. Use the names for references.
The Level corresponds to the level of favor or opposition one would expect from the character as well as the deals they might offer during [[Trading Rules|Trading]].
