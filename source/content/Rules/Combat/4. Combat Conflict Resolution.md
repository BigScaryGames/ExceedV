## Attacking and Defending

All offensive effects have one or more traits of #Strike, #Projectile, #Burst, #Mind, #Body. Those determine which defenses can be used against them.

### Combat Resolution Sequence

1. **Attacker declares the attack** — target, weapon or spell, and its trait(s).
2. **Defender declares the defense** — one of the defenses the attack's traits allow.
3. **Attacker declares pre-roll boosts** — stances, abilities, spent reactions.
4. **Defender declares pre-roll boosts** — defensive abilities, buffs, reactions.
5. **Both roll.**
6. **Resolve the outcome** — damage or effect.

**Defending is free.** Deflect, Dodge, Resolve and Endure cost no AP and never require your Reaction — every incoming attack can be defended. Reactions are spent only where an ability says so.

### Ranged Combat

![[Ranged Combat]]

**Unopposed:** Roll defense against a static DC (traps, environmental hazards, ongoing effects)
**Opposed:** Roll defense against attacker's roll (creature attacks, targeted spells)

Attack and defense rolls follow standard check rules from [[Core Resolution System]]: 2d10 + Skill + Attribute (+ Bonuses/Penalties).
