The maps in Exceed are defined in Lines and Zones. Maps can be used with or without a grid, as distances are tracked with lines — no map at all, or a map on a napkin, works as well as any other map.

A creature's position amounts to two things: its whereabouts (what Zone it is in), and who it's contending or shielding with in melee (its [[Skirmish]]).

# Zones

Zones are abstract areas — a room, an alleyway, a hilltop, a city square. The GM decides the zoning based on the needs of the scene.

Within a zone, you can get into a [[Skirmish]] with an enemy or an ally with a single [[Engage]] action, followed by an attack or other offensive ability.

When you [[Engage]] an enemy or an ally — or they engage you — you engage all the targets in that [[Skirmish]].

# Lines

Lines represent the distance between zones. Each line has a Distance statistic and can hold effects.

**Line of sight:** blocking line of sight is a property of a line (a wall, a corner, thick smoke). Generally, whether a zone can be seen from another is up to the GM.

`Cost of traversal = Distance × Multipliers from Effects`
`AP to Move = ceil(Cost / Speed)`

You must have the full AP cost available to cross — there is no partial movement along a line. GMs are advised to write the costs on the map next to each line to keep track of the effects.

! Rule of thumb
Lines should cost no more than 20 in the late game and no more than 10 in the early game. When designing a map, no zone should be further than about 2/3 of a turn of [[Move]] from its neighbors on average.

To calculate distance between disconnected zones reference [[Distance in Combat]].

# Effects

Lines and Zones can hold effects that modify the rules — providing effects, abilities and alike.

Environmental, situational or magical — some can be removed, suppressed or bypassed by abilities explicitly stating so, or with checks and improvisation approved by the GM.

Examples:
Line is a wall, requiring DC 15 climbing check and distance 10.
*"Hey GM, I want to cast levitate on the group" / "Hey GM, I want to be a big guy and help others climb by jump-starting them" / "Hey GM, can I throw my rope dart and climb that way"*
*"Cast the spell / Lower the DC by 5 if you spend your whole turn on this / Add an extra AP, and reduce DC by 3."*
