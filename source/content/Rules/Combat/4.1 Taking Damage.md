Players take damage as per [[HP And Wounds|Health and Wounds]] first depleting stamina, then depleting health. Players can reach negative HP with a value of Max Health not accounted for wounds.

Healing restores the pools in reverse order: **Health first, then Stamina.**
## Getting Below 0 HP and Death

**Getting to 0 Health:** **Each** time your Health reaches or goes below 0, roll a [[Consequence Roll]] , and mark 1 wound.

When at or below 0 Health you need to roll Endure vs Treatment DC of your highest treatment DC wound to stay conscious at the start of each of your turns.

- Each **lethal hit** you take in this state makes you roll a [[Consequence Roll]].
- Each **non-lethal hit** you take in this state makes you roll Endure against the hit to stay conscious.



**Death:** You die when your **lethal** wounds would exceed your Max Wounds, or when **lethal** damage takes you below negative full Health. 

**Unconcious from negative HP** If the attack that brings you below **negative full health** is nonlethal you fall unconcious. 
You won't regain conciousness untill you reach +1Health.

**Unconcious from nonlethal wounds** -  If your wounds exceed max wounds, but lethal wounds don't, you go unconscious.
You won't regain conciousness untill you regain at least one wound and reach +1 Health.
