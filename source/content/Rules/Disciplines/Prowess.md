**Type:** Discipline
**XP Pool:** Battle XP

Combat skill in Exceed is determined by one's **Prowess Discipline**.

**Prowess Perks** (`#Prowess`): Fighting techniques, weapon training, tactical abilities — their XP advances the Prowess discipline. Paid with Battle XP.

## Progression

Prowess advances through XP spent on **#Prowess** perks (including weapon training). All such XP accumulates toward the tier thresholds — the discipline is never bought directly. Battle XP spent on Conditioning perks or on battle perks with no discipline tag does not advance Prowess.

Prowess perks show **(Attribute/Attribute)** for XP contribution when purchased.
