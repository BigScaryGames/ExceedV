# Weapons

## Building a Weapon
(There will be some weapon presets, but i'll make a builder in the app).

First select the number of hands and the weight.
Heavy adds a trait point, Two-handed increases the dice size by 1 step.
Then select the weapon tier (your starting or the one you find).
Tier 0 starts with 1 point. Each tier after gets an extra point.


Then select the base Type - Blades/Thrown/Polearm/Brawling etc.

Start adding traits. From tier 4 and on weapon abilities are TBD - endgame content that is MS9.

Spells that give bonuses to the owner inside weapons, like in other items, are using Limit, not necceceraly traits. #TBD but design intent is to put specific buffs that against Limit not traits, that are not modifying the weapon but the wielder.

Always-on traits do not use Limit unless the trait says otherwise.

### Weapon Categories

Having training in any listed category allows you to use the weapon without the -2 untrained penalty.

| Category | Weapons |
|----------|---------|
| **Blades** | Daggers, swords, rapiers, greatswords |
| **Polearms** | Spears, staves, pikes, glaives, halberds |
| **Axes** | Axes, picks, cleavers |
| **Impact** | Maces, hammers, clubs, flails, staffs |
| **Brawling** | Fists, kicks, grappling |
| **Bows** | Bows, crossbows |
| **Thrown** | Javelins, darts, throwing axes |
| **Shield** | Shield boss |

Thrown weapons use the same range increments as other ranged weapons (Tier × 10 m). The Aim action comes from Bow training — Thrown training alone doesn't grant it, but a bow-trained thrower can Aim thrown attacks. Traits cannot be taken twice until explicitly mentioned in the trait.

| --------------- | ----------------- | ------------- | ----------------- | ------------ | ---------- | ------ |
| Buckler         | —                 | 0             | 1                 | 2            | Light      | 0.5    |
| Round Shield    | —                 | 0             | 1                 | 3            | Medium     | 3      |
| Kite Shield     | 1                 | 1             | 2                 | 4            | Medium     | 3      |
| Tower Shield    | 2                 | 2             | 3                 | 5            | Heavy      | 6      |
| Fortress Shield | 3/-1              | 3             | 4                 | 6            | Heavy      | 8      |

**Shield Deflection:**
- Light and medium shields can use Agility when Deflecting
- Medium and heavy shields can use Strength when Deflecting
- This allows medium shields to be used with either Agility or Strength

**Armor Penalty:** Combines with worn armor.
Defense bonus from shield applies to deflect; some perks allow expanding the bonus to other defenses.

## Consumables
Tier 0-5 Spells can be made into potions and scrolls. Scrolls require a Magery perk or Arcane Theology skill; potions do not. Don't forget oils.
## Mundane Equipment
Tools, supplies, and trade goods are available for purchase. For full price tables — weapons, armor, gear, animals, services, potions and scrolls — see [[Prices]].

Availability: common goods are in stock in any market town; exotic goods, masterwork wares and anything magical are a story, not a shopping trip.
