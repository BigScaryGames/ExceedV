# 3. Creating a Character

## Step 1: Concept
Decide on a name and concept for your character.

## Step 2: Starting XP
GM declates starting Experience Points (XP) in two categories: 

| XP Type       | Used For                                                                                            |
| ------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Battle XP** | #Prowess perks, Conditioning perks, #Magery perks, spells, and Battle perks with no discipline tag. |
| **Life XP**   | Social, knowledge, crafting, wilderness [[Skills]], and Skill perks                                 |
The GM also provides with limits to how many points worth of flaws a character can take. The generic advice it to limit flaws to 1/3 of the points with 1/2 in case of very low points start. More on that in [[Perks and Flaws#How Flaws Work|How Flaws Work]]

| Flaw limit  | Used For                                                     |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Battle Flaw | Flaws that affect combat.                                    |
| Life Flaw  | Flaws that affect social, exploraion or downtime activities. |

## Step 3: Spend XP
Spend XP on skills, perks, and spells. Each purchase shows (Attribute1) or (Attribute1/Attribute2) indicating which attributes can benefit. When making a purchase, you choose one of the listed attributes to apply the XP toward. When attributes reach tier thresholds (10/30/60/100/150), you gain +1 to that attribute, up to +5.
Voluntary flaws work in a similar manner, but instead reduce an attribute and give you XP you can spend in said category.

**Movement Speed** (in-game referenced as just "Speed"):
Calculate your movement speed - this will be used both in-combat and in exploration and chase scenes.

`Speed = 5 + Agility + Bonuses - Penalties`

## Progression Costs

### Skills
[[Skills]] - progressed by paying cost based on their [[Skill Complexity Price|complexity]].

### Domains
Domains (such as Magery or Prowess) follow different progression rules - they are progressed by learning domain abilities (perks and spells).

| Domain         | Progression        | Method                                                     |
| -------------- | ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Magery** | 10/+20/+30/+40/+50 | Learning spells and #Magery perks (gated by skills, see [[6. Magic System]]) |
| **Prowess**    | 10/+20/+30/+40/+50 | Learning #Prowess perks                                     |

Some perks apply to multiple domains. When learning such a perk, choose which domain receives the XP (similar to choosing attributes).
