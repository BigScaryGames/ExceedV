## Skills System

Skills are the result of the life experience of your character. The result of dedication and/or talent and time spent mastering them.
They are split into Easy, Average, Hard by their difficulty to train and master. Next to the skills they are marked with (E)(A)(H).

At level 2-4 some of the skills offer Focus - a skill modification that you can decide to choose or not (sometimes the choise is mandatory).

- Easy and Average -  at level 3
- Hard - at level 2

They for example - allow counting the skill level higher in one field and lower in other, may grant bonuses or abilities, etc.

When taking Focus skills are written as for example - Medicine (H) (Surgeon) [3].

![[Skill Complexity Price]]

Changing the Focus can be done during the skill improvement, otherwise it requires half the time and training as to learn the current level of the skill. Look at [[Training Rules]].

## Natural Sciences

Skills in Natural Sciences don't have option to not select Focus. From third level on, checks of the skill that are in another field are locked on level two.

You can add Focuss to your science skills (and gain the appropriate benefits) by training [[Scientific Breath]] perk. It can be taken multiple times, adding a new Focus each time.

Sciences are often overlapping e.g. Pharmacy, Alchemy and Microbiology, if you have an adjacent skill you may roll the adjacent one with a disadvantage. If you have both the skill and the adjacent skill - e.g. when investigating a ritual that creates magical disease and you have both Magical Science and Medical Science, ask the GM to consider giving you an advantage on the roll.

## A Life Skill

Not every human activity requires a skill attached. If you want a skill that lies outside the Focuss and categorie - ask the GM if it is reasonable to add a livelihood skill.
Livelihood skills have no fixed attribute pair or complexity; the GM assigns them on creation. They can be used to earn [[Earning Income|Income]] they in normal circumstances.
They are not put in the same bucket as other, more adventuring related skills, for they are more focused on earning livelihood and rarely come up during adventuring or are plainly nieche. More often than not they might require a working facility (a farm, a smithy, a building to construct) or a specific setting (Courtroom, river, a town with customers).
Examples of skills by complexity.
1. **Easy Skills** - Mining, Logging, Construction, Skinning, Furnishing, Cooking, Blacksmithing, Fishing, Farming. Riding
2. **Average Skills** - Guarding, Tailor, Armoring, Weaponsmithing, Leatherworking, Masonry, Seafaring, Peddling.
3. **Complex Skills** - Lawyer, Accounting, Jewelcrafting, Tax collection, Painting (pictures, not walls), Sculpting, Poetry.

The examples are not limited to the list, when a craft is absent, ask the GM to determine its skill complexity and assign it the attributes.
Expressive art is usually in Professional Skills (H) category, but that requires GM clarification.
