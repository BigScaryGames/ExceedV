A young drake that decided your cause is its hoard. Feed it well.

```
Drake Whelp | Threat 3 | #melee #caster (bond-compatible)
Init 12 | Speed 6 (flying 8) | commands: Move/Engage 1 AP, Bite 3 AP, Breath 4 AP

Deflect 16 | Dodge 14 | Resolve 13 | Endure 16
Stamina 28 (no Health — Bond Breaks at 0)

— Bite | 3 AP | #Strike | 2d10+5 vs defense roll | d10+4
— Breath | 4 AP | #Burst | your zone or one zone connected by a line, once per combat | DC 14 vs Dodge | 2d6 of its element, halves on success
— Instinct: pride before survival — it will answer a challenge
```

Its element is chosen when bonded (fire, frost, storm, ash). Its Breath writes the zone with its element's trait for a turn — feeds the MS9 elemental zone rules.

**Bond 4–5:** Stamina +14, Bite 2 dice, Breath 2d10 and twice per combat. A drake is a commitment — expect the GM to remember what drakes owe and are owed.
