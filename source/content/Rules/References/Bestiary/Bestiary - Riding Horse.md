A warhorse that will hold a line, not just carry you over one.

```
Riding Horse | Threat 1 | #mount #melee (bond-compatible)
Init 10 | Speed 8 | commands: Move/Engage 2 AP, Trample 3 AP

Deflect 12 | Dodge 12 | Resolve 9 | Endure 14
Stamina 18 (no Health — Bond Breaks at 0)

— Hooves | 2 AP | #Strike | 2d10+1 vs defense roll | d6+2 | Nonlethal
— Trample | 3 AP | #Strike | Move through the target's zone, one #Strike vs Dodge | d8+1 | Large
— Instinct: holds when held; refuses only obvious, lethal folly
```

As a mount: while you ride, your Move costs use its Speed 8, and you count as one combatant with it for [[Skirmish|numeric advantage]]. Disengaging from a ridden horse's skirmish requires defeating its Deflect or eating a free hoof strike.

**Bond 4–5:** Stamina +10, Trample d10+2, and it may make one free hoof strike per turn against anyone who attacks you while mounted.
