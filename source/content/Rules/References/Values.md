Player characters have values that are not scored.

NPC's on the other hand have values scored on six axises.  −5..+5. 

These are value-based approximation of their characters, stipped of nuance.
The amount of pronounced characteristics depends on dimensionality of the character and the strengh of their opinions, their vividness. 

0-1 Subtle  - means that the character is slighly leaning towards something and doesn't hold a firm opinion.
2-3 Pronounced- means that the character finds the values imporatant enough to change own behavior and life around them.
4-5 Major- this is for people who have very strong, usually battle-tested opinoins that dictate their lives.

| +Hierarchy - Egality         | The view on rank and status.                                                                |

When writing down just write as "Bravery 2" or "Hedonism 3" for comfort of reading.  Not Ascetism -3.

## A quick explaination on values.

**Order** is about structuring of own life and the world around, while **chaos** is lack or descruction of structures and order. 

**Assertiveness** is being direct, often ambitions and willing to go against societal pressure. 
**Conformity** is about being more tactful, agreeable, cooperative and polite.

## Traits
Things like Trusting, Suspicious, Vengeful and Forgiving are not values but traits. 
They are mentioned only if highly pronounced.
