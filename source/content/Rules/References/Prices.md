All prices are in **Silver(S)**.  
(Think 10$ per Silver piece. 200 pieces per Kilo. We don't count weight of money, unless talking about ludacrious abount of money in weigh, or robberier and alike).

Prices assume a functioning market town — wilderness markups, shortages and sieges are the GM's discression. E.g. GM might deem a town Touristy - and double Shelter and Dining out prices, or wartorn and lower the weapon and armor prices by 20%. 
#TBD this is just reference points. Items can have quiality "Cheap/Used" for lower price and penalty while using or malfunctions on criticals - e.g; shoddy sword might snap on a critical failure to parry.  
## Weapons
Weapons cost based on their tier. 
Thrown and Expandable weapons cost **1/10th** of their tiered price, (returning takes away this, as they stop being expandable).
If a weapon is Two handed or Heavy **2x** the price (doesn't stack).

Game doesn't count simple ammunition unless in special circumstances.
