Whether conducting negotiations, gathering gossip, conning someone, or talking your way out of jail in court, social interactions follow a set of rules.
Social interactions operate in two modes. 
- **Social Interaction** — casual interactions are mainly cicling around Disposition - the standing with a person. Disposition −5..+5 clarifies your relationship level with the other and answers the question on *what you can ask for and be asked for*. 
-This is mainly for GMs to track. 
- **Social Combat Subsystem** TBD — formal debates, court intrigue match, any real-time contested exchange. This is a battle with winning conditions and uses Lines and Zones. [TBD]

# Social Interactions
During a social interaction scene you interact normally - based on roleplay logic with malleable rules to streamline the mechanical aspects of the interactions.

At the start of the first back and forth - the [[First Impression]] is setting the disposition point value. As mentioned in first impression Players might attempt to improve the [[First Impression]] during this. 

Then during the social interaction players can attempt to **assess** the **values** (below), **disposition** (below) and **weaknesses** (below) of the others. **Impress** them to change their disposition and **Suggest** them a course of action or ask for favors.

## GM Guidelines

Most calculations happen prior to rolls. Use Appearance, value representation, race, rank difference, and similar factors to set initial disposition.

**First meeting:** Typically -2 to 2 POINTS range. 
- NPCs don't care much about PCs most of the time,

## Example Character
Danny - organizer of Brine town events. He is 13 years young but opinionated. He likes when things are done properly, he knows little aside of stories about outsiders. Despite being born into the second richest family in Brine he doesn't see people through their place in society of the village. He doesn't understand that yet, but he dislikes the world of adults where people are in ladders and classes, while using organizational structures he sees them as tools, not weights to weight worthiness of a person.
**Order** 2, **Egality** 2, **Trusting**

Sir Brandon - is a knight who loves remembering his good old days in military as he sits looking at the sea from the cliffs of Brine. He liked when people understood their place and behaved as they should.  **Hierarchy** 3. But after leaving military he is a not well self-organized man, loving to drink and sleep in from time to time **Hedonism** 1. He has an idea of who are his ingroup - Humans, and he likes them way more than pointy ears or blue skins. **Ingroup** 2.
When writing the character write as **Hierarchy** 3, **Ingroup** 2 and drop the minor hedonism as a lesser value. 

Human Supremacy Party Leader - He loves his people **Ingroup** 4, though might tolerate other folk while they are outside the borders of human nations. As a fanatic he knows one of the green or blue skins might attack him one day, he pretends to not care, but he is slightly paranoid, but not a coward to run away. Trait - **Suspicious**. He thinks that humans should be in a strict **Hierarchy 4** where people follow the nobility like him, who rules them, and there is no confusion about rank and status, so people would follow their lot instead of trying to fly to the sun only to burn his wings (so no order). He is not a particularly well organized person, neither he seeks to regiment humans, after all he likes his fellow humans enough to trust them 
Writing him is **Ingroup** 4, **Hierarchy** 4, **Suspicious**.
