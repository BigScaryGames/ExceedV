A group in a zone that are in melee range with each other. A single character can't be in 2 engagements at the same time.

## Strength in numbers 
Unless a zone states otherwise having a numerical disadvantage within a skirmish provides -1 to deflect and dodge rolls per difference to the defender capped at -4. The cap applies to the numeric-advantage penalty only — other penalties (e.g. [[Condition - Off Guard|Off-Guard]]) stack beyond it.

**Back-to-back:** if you have at least 3 allies in your skirmish (or count as having 3 allies), you no longer suffer the numeric disadvantage on defense.

*Example: 2 guards skirmish 5 bandits — each guard deflects and dodges at -3 (outnumbered 5 to 2). Two more guards join: each of the 4 guards now has 3 allies in the skirmish, so back-to-back applies and they defend with no numeric penalty despite still being outnumbered.*

Reach and Range weapons allow attacking without entering the skirmish, the attacks count the attacker for the benefit of numeric advantage for that attack.

To disengage from the [[Skirmish]] you need to perform a [[Disengage]] action. Trying to Leave the [[Skirmish]] with or without a [[Disengage]] action can interact with an ability of an effect.
