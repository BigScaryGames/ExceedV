#Zonal #effect
Requires activation. The first team to take control of the zone (entering it uncontested, or driving the previous holders out) may activate this effect: the holding team gains a +2 #Situational bonus to Deflect and Dodge against attacks coming from outside the zone (cover, barricades, arrow slits).

The effect ends if the holders lose control of the zone; the new holders may activate it in turn, unless the fortifications were destroyed.
