In combat, spend 2 AP to shout a tactical command. Each teammate who follows it verbatim on their next turn rolls that action with **+1 Morale** (their choice which action; must be reasonable — 'attack the engineer' works, 'win' doesn't).

Outside combat, when leading a collective task, the group adds your full Leadership (or relevant skill) bonus while your personal contribution counts at half — you are the conductor, not the violin.
