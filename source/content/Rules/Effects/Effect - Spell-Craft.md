You can invent new spells through research: a downtime project of weeks (GM sets the length by ambition), requiring a library or laboratory, Magical Theory >= the intended tier, and XP equal to the spell's tier cost on success. Roll Magical Theory at project's end vs DC 12 + 2×tier — failure wastes the time, not the theory: the next attempt on the same design gets +2.
GM can decline this perk from being learned, If the perk is taken but GM declines to make spells - it is refunded with downtime.
