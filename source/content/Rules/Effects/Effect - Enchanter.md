You can perform routine upkeep on enchanted items during downtime: recharge limited-use items, refresh a fading permanent effect for another season, or re-attune an item to a new owner — a day's work plus materials per [[Prices]]. Creating new enchantments or altering an item's fundamental enchantment is a different tier of craft entirely, and waits for the full crafting rules.
