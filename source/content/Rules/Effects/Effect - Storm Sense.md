Each evening with open sky, roll Survival against DC 10 to read tomorrow's weather. GM rolls 1d10 - on 1 the prediction is wrong due to changing weather.
On a success the GM states the next day's weather. If the party then camps or plans the day around your forecast, that weather treats the party one step milder: route difficulty caused by it drops one step, and the party gains no Fatigue from it unless it worsens. A wrong forecast gives no sign that it was wrong.
