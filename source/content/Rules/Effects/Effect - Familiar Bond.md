A familiar is bound to you by magic, not by training. It occupies **1 Limit slot** while present — banishing it frees the slot, summoning it back costs 1 AP.

- The familiar is a Tiny creature ([[Size Rules]]); use the [[Bestiary - Hunting Hawk|Hunting Hawk]] statblock or a GM-approved Tiny spirit.
- You share senses: while the familiar is within one zone of you, you may use its Perception in place of yours.
- A familiar has no commands and acts on its own instinct (scout, watch, perch) — commanding it in combat follows [[Bonded Companions]] with Command Cap 1.
