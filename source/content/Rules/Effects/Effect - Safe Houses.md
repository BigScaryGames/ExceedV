If you have spend a tenday in a city or a town you know places to lay low.
In an urban area you can produce a safe place to lie low for a d4 number of days — abandoned tenement, dementia suffering widow's cellar, criminal backroom. 
Attempt to asses your or party's location has  your Streetwise as a penalty for the seekers.
Once the place is overused it requires either prolongation, finding a new place by spending a day/night or can lead to a complication.
