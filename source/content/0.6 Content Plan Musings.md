# 0.6 Content Plan Musings

**Status:** Musings — playtest the v0.6 Lines and Zones core first; generate content only after the system proves out.

## Core idea

Don't invent new mechanics — give players **write-access to the existing effect menu**. [[4.0 Lines and Zones]] already defines the line-effect types (modified cost, check, hazardous check, barrier, enemy-created) and zone-effect types (defensive advantage, hazard, ability modifiers, magical). A "tunnel of flame" is not a new system; it's a spell that writes *Hazardous check* onto a line. A tank in a doorway writes *Barrier*. Content generation becomes combinatorics + numbers.

## Design rules (lock these before generating)

1. **Anchored vs free-standing — the martial/caster split.**
	- Martial control is *anchored*: the effect exists while your body stands there, and consumes your Reaction as upkeep. Removed by moving the body — the Shoving line is the anti-tank kit.
	- Magic control is *free-standing*: costs Limit or duration. Removed by dispel/consume (future elemental consume ladder, MS9) or by eating the check.
2. **Reaction = the control currency.** Hold-the-line stances, overwatch, ready strikes — all keyed to the single Reaction. Self-caps how much one character can lock down. [[Ability - Tower Defender|Tower Defender]] and [[Ability - Hair Trigger|Hair Trigger]] already prototype this.
3. **Counterplay on the card.** Every placed effect states its removal line (kill/move the anchor, check DC, damage threshold, dispel). Mandatory template field.
4. **Cap the graffiti.** One effect per source per line/zone — otherwise maps become a spreadsheet (the thing the no-DOTs ruling exists to avoid).
5. **Anti-turtle pricing.** Control must cost more up front than the AP it denies per turn, paying off over rounds. Attacker answers must exist and be priced: Shove/[[Herding Strike]], siege gear vs [[Fortified Defenders]], #Burst spells ignoring barriers.

## Starter set (build after playtest)

Martial:
- **Hold the Line** (Shield) — stance: the line you guard becomes a Barrier; crossing = opposed check vs you or eat a strike
- **Overwatch** — generic [[Ready]] variant: cover a line, first enemy crossing eats a strike; perk upgrades for archery (multi-shot) and polearms (cheaper readies)
- **Barricade** — interact action + props; the martial "wall spell"

Spells:
- **Tunnel of Flame** (T1-2) — line: Hazardous check; tags the zone #Fire (feeds MS9 elemental zone traits)
- **Ice Sheet / Grease** — line: cost multiplier + check to cross
- **Obscuring Fog** — zone: Perception penalty + blocks LoS on its lines
- **Wall of Stone** — line: Barrier with damage threshold
- **Sanctuary** — zone: entry requires Resolve check (ritual cousin of [[Just the Two of Us]])

## Playtest questions (what "the system works" means)

- Do skirmish flips (duel ↔ skirmish) and numeric advantage produce interesting decisions, or does everyone just stack one skirmish?
- Are line costs felt? Does anyone ever choose the long way around?
- Does the back-to-back rule read at the table without re-explaining?
- Is one Reaction enough currency for control play to feel real but not oppressive?
- Does the Shove ladder actually get used as counterplay, or is killing the anchor always better?
