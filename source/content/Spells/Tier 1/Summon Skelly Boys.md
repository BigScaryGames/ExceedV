**Base Target/Range:** Your zone

**Limit Cost:** 1 per servant
**Duration:** Until unraveled or dismissed

## Effect
Raise **2 Skeletal Servants** ([[Bestiary - Skeletal Servant]]) from loose bones or fresh corpses in your zone. They follow all [[6.1 Summoning|minion rules]]: they act on your turn with the mook economy, inherit your boons, unravel at 0 Stamina, and never crit.

Grave-robbing not included. Corpses may be provided by the caster.

## Description
The classic first step into necromancy: the dead are cheap, obedient, and beyond disappointment.
