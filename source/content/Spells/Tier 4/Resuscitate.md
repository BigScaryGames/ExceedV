**Requirements:** [[Regeneration]], [[Life and Death]]
**Tier:** 4
**AP Cost:** 5
**Attributes:** WL/CH
**Base Target/Range:** 1 dead target
**Traits:** #Spell #Active #Healing
**Limit Cost:** -
**Duration:** Instant

## Effect
Allows healing of the dead body to lower Life and Death check difficulty, or allow it in case of injuries incompatible with life. Failure = the spell doesn't work.
