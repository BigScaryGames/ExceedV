Levitate targets to move them through the air

**Limit Cost:** 3
**Effect:** Each AP spent moves 1 target within its zone; moving it to a connected zone costs 1 AP. Any successful attack against you will require an Endure check or your ability to hold the spell falters.

## Advanced Version

**Effect:** Each AP spent moves 1 target or the whole team within their zone; moving them to a connected zone costs 1 AP. Any successful attack against you will require an Endure check or your ability to hold the spell falters.
