**Requirements:** [[Elemental Weapon]], [[Summon Weapon]]
**Tier:** 2
**AP Cost:** 1
**Attributes:** WL/AG
**Base Target/Range:** Self
**Traits:** #Spell #Attuned #Conjuration #Equipment
**Limit Cost:** 1
**Duration:** Until the end of the encounter

## Effect
As [[Summon Weapon]], but the weapon burns with one element you control: its damage gains **+[Magery] elemental damage** of that element, and every third hit may push the element's trait onto the victim's zone (MS9 elemental zone rules).

Only one elemental weapon at a time; summoning a new one dismisses the old. You are still holding a burning (or crackling, or frost-rimed) stick of your own will — mind your gloves.

## Description
The impatient duelist's enchantment: why pay a smith when your Will holds the temper?
