**Requirements:** Magery 2, [[Elemental Strike]]
**Tier:** 2
**AP Cost:** 5
**Attributes:** WL/CH
**Base Target/Range:** Your zone
**Traits:** #Spell #Active #Attuned #Conjuration
**Limit Cost:** 2
**Duration:** Until unraveled or dismissed

## Effect
Summon a **Threat 2 elemental** of one element you can name from its raw presence in the zone ([[Effect - Elemental Abundance|Elemental Abundance]] counts). Build it on the [[NPC and Encounter Builder|NPC builder]] (rolling NPC, 3 AP acting on your turn, no reactions) or ask the GM for the house elemental.

It follows all [[6.1 Summoning|minion rules]] — with a real NPC's economy instead of the mook economy. If it unravels, the Limit slots free only after a breather.
