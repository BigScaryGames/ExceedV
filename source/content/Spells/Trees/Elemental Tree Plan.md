
## Elements
All mages can learn Minor Elemental Manipulation and from there control spells of the basic elements.  You can change your mage's elemental composition by narrowing it down to purely elemental, or selecting a limitation for your mage.
This will narrow your spellcrafting abilities in exchange for more of the same. (MS8 TBD)
Learning complex elements is a perk.
Element Modifiers are coming in ms8. 
### Basic Elements

| Element   | Damage Type          | Element Mod                              | Manipulation Notes                    |
| --------- | -------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Fire      | Heat                 | Bigger Die                               | Ignite, shape, extinguish flames      |
| Water     | Slashing/Bludgeoning | Weakness to cold, Electricity. Nonlethal | Create, purify, shape water           |
| Earth     | Bludgeoning          | Projectile.                              | Shape earth/stone, create barriers    |
| Air       | Slashing             | counts as AOE.                           | Gusts, deflect projectiles, suffocate |
| Cold      | Cold                 | Slowing (rules tbd)                      | Freeze, create ice surfaces/weaponry  |
| Lightning | Electricity          | Stunning (rules tbs)                     | Storms, stun, power devices           |
