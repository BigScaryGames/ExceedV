**Requirements:** -
**Tier:** 0
**AP Cost:** brather
**Attributes:** CH/WL
**Base Target/Range:** Up to 5 sentient beings
**Traits:** #Ritual

Mage can conduct a breather-long Ritual with willing targets to form a team or include someone in a team. This allows mage to apply team effects on his allies while they are in his zone or a connected zone; allies lose the effects while away and regain them on return. 
This also allows the mage to cast harmful effects on his allies without resistance. 

To break away from the team you need to spend a full round on your turn and succeed a Resolve check against the ritualist's Magical Theory. Some state that the ritual is built that way, so parties won't screw supports over loot.
