**Requirements:** -
**Attributes:** Varies
**Cost:** -Varies XP (Life Flaw)
**Tags:** #Flaw

You are depended on something. It might be a drug need to spend daily 2 hours under the sunlight to survive.
Unlike addictions your agency is not taken away. When not fulfilling the need you take damage.
Value and attributes are based on type (commonality and accessibility) and strenght of the dependency. 
Normally this is taken as needs from chronic illness or supernatural affliction.
