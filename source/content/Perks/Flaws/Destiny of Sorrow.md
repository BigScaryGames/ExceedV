## Grants
You are meant to play a role, a minor or a major in an event fortold, and a diviner meeting you would know of you being tied by one.
You are guaranteed a tragic experience, and circumstances pushing towards a mistake that would cost you dearly.
It might strip you of status, wealth or even your life. 
This perk can't be bought off, only desitiny fulfilled or destiny broken. 
You might be destined to die, destined to see your wolrd burn or see your house fall. When you encounter you destiny you have a choice to reject or fullfill it.
When fullfilling the Destiny of Sorrow all your checks will be rolled with advantage. When rejecting the destiny - with disadvantage. The advantage and disadvantage cannot be negated.
