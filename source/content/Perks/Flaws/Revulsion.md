**Attributes:** EN

**Tags:** #Flaw

You have a supernatural reaction to something normal for others. Like a vampire with garlic, or a demon to cross, being in direct presence of that something makes you roll flat roll  at the start of each turn to not be [[Condition - Shaken]] or not to increase the value of your Shaken condition. 
Shaken won't decrease unless you disengage with the source of Revulsion.
The Value is based on flat DC roll - higher = more expensive and commonality/knownness of the element. Revulsion must be connected to who you are, and should be possible to deduce or test.
