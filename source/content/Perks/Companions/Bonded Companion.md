## Description
THIS IS #TBD You have a companion bound to you — a warhound, a horse, a hawk, a bear. Choose a bond-compatible entry from the [[Bestiary]]. It has no AP and no initiative of its own: you command it on your turn, paying its costs from your AP, up to a **Command Cap of 2** actions per turn. It scales with the higher of your Magery or Prowess, fights on Stamina alone, and the [[Bonded Companions|bond rules]] decide the rest. Companion perks advance no domain.
