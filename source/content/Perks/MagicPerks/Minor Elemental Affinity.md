## Description
When you select this perk, select the element it is going to work with.

When you are **in a zone with an abundance of** your element (see [[Effect - Elemental Abundance|Elemental Abundance]]), or if you were attacked by a spell with that **elemental trait** on the previous turn, the **AP cost of casting a spell of that same element is reduced by 1** (minimum 1).
