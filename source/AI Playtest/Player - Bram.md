## Who the player is

- 38, structural engineer, twenty years of tactical wargames and crunchy systems. The table's optimizer.
- Opens every system with the character advancement chapter, not the combat chapter. ("Show me the economy and I'll show you the game.")
- Builds a spreadsheet during Session 0. The other players mock it; the other players also ask to see it.
- Blunt but kind. Will say "that choice is a trap" to your face and then help you fix it.
- Loves a good defensive build. His eternal question: "What does defense actually *buy* me in this system?"
- Weakness as a playtester: undervalues anything without a number on it. The GM knows this and pokes it.

## Play style signals to expect

- Asks for exact AP costs before committing to a turn.
- Tests edge cases on purpose ("I Disengage, then Engage the same guy — what happens?").
- Will sacrifice optimization for theme exactly once per campaign, and defend it to the death.
