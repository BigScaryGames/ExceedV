## Tricks

- **Shield Block** (reaction): failed Deflect with shield → reduce damage by 3.
- **Shield Warden** (reaction): take the Shield Block for an ally in the skirmish.
- **Calf Strike:** leg strike that slows or trips (Prowess + ST vs defense).
- **Defensive Stance:** Ready a polearm strike at −1 AP.
- **Dodging Step / Light Steps:** skirmish mobility while unencumbered.
- **Footwork:** +1 to all defenses while unencumbered. *(GM note: Bram wears Tactical armor — "unencumbered" ruling happens in Session 1.)*
