## Who the player is

- 29, secondary-school literature teacher. Came up on narrative-first systems; this is her second crunchy game ever.
- Plays for character first, always. Asks "what would she do?" before "what's the optimal action?"
- Allergic to jargon acronyms. If the table says "AP" and "DC" in the same sentence without explaining, she drifts.
- Bold in fiction: the first to talk to the scary NPC, the first to touch the weird artifact. Justifies it as "story tax".
- Genuinely wants to love the magic system; judges it on whether spells feel like *rituals and craft* or like a menu.
- Weakness as a playtester: loses track of resources mid-fight (Strain, Limit, reactions). Whatever she forgets is a UX finding, not a player failure — she insists on this, loudly.

## Play style signals to expect

- Names her spells. Calls Elemental Strike "the kettle" because of the hiss.
- Pushes for social scenes to matter mechanically ("did my Gossip actually change his attitude number?").
- Will attempt something illegal once per session and accept "no" gracefully.
