## Who the player is

- 33, night-shift nurse. Plays to decompress; sixteen years of irregular attendance across a dozen systems.
- Rogue loyalist. If a system lets him hide, slip behind somebody, climb a wall, and lie to a guard, he's happy.
- "Rule of cool" negotiator: pitches three improvised stunts a session. Accepts "roll for it" as praise.
- Hates bookkeeping with a passion. Any mechanic that makes him track a number every round gets side-eyed in feedback.
- Surprisingly tactical when it counts — night shift teaches triage. Good instinct for "who's about to drop".
- Weakness as a playtester: never reads the rulebook twice. If he misremembers a rule, that misremembering is data.

## Play style signals to expect

- Volunteers for every scouting mission, then complains about being alone out there.
- Befriends NPCs the plot meant to be throwaway.
- Keeps a running joke going until the GM regretfully kills it.
