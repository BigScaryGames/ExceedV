An AI-simulated playtest of the Exceed v0.6 rules, run end-to-end by four maintained identities: a Game Master and three TRPG players, each with a matching in-game character. Every die in the adventure was rolled by script (Python RNG) — never picked by the narrator — and every improvised ruling was logged for the designer.

## The findings

- **[[Playtest Statistics]]** — full roll ledger, dice-fairness audit (122 d10s), damage and XP economy, action-economy observations.
- **[[System Analysis - Findings]]** — consolidated strengths, weaknesses, the 15-ruling gap ledger, balance alarms, and what the next playtest should target.

**One-line takeaway:** the tactical core (AP economy, rolled defenses, Lines and Zones, encounter builder) held up on first contact; the magic math (crit rate, Strain, 20-spell menus) and the attribute-threshold economy (near-misses, gated perks) are where the next design pass should aim.
